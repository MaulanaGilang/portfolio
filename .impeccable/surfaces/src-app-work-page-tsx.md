Scope: the portfolio home page (`/`), mode Experience. Case-study pages inherit the world.

Audience and job: recruiters for data roles decide in one screen whether to read on; action is email, LinkedIn or résumé; proof is the five projects and the role outcomes.

Chosen direction: pinned by the user from four references (yinger.dev opening, MECHA type and layout, INVERSA labels and aerial image, Terminal Industries dusk), palette from the Dune Part One and Part Two posters. No roll: a user-pinned direction beats it. Build path: code-led (paid image generation is off the table).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one day over a dune field. Data blocks lie half-buried in Part One's mist; scrolling lifts them out of the sand and files them into an ordered structure, and the page ends in Part Two's spice dusk. Refuses the split hero with a portrait and a row of skill cards.

OWN-WORLD: Mist canvas #e6edeb, haze #d3e0dd, sand #e7dccb, ink #221e1d, stone #4f5a55, Caladan teal #5e8b8b for marks, spice #b23a10 / #d35c11 as the one accent; night #170504 with sun #ea7d0c and gold #efc650. Dune Rise on the name and the closing words only; Satoshi for everything read; Geist Mono for data and readouts. Pill controls, 4px media, hairlines, no cards in cards.

STORY: Visitors see the name and the target role over a living dune field, understand "raw data in, trusted data out" by watching it happen, read the work, and leave through a clear contact close.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed WebGL dune field in mist with scattered half-buried blocks; top-left role line, one-sentence intro and a résumé pill; bottom edge GILANG MAULANA in Dune Rise at full width; readouts small at the right. Primary action: Résumé, with Let's talk in the nav.

FORM: user-pinned composite, position 1 on the list. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the hero pins while scroll lifts blocks from the sand into a filed structure (reduced motion shows the filed state). Motion grammar: slow exponential ease-outs, one scrubbed moment, no per-section bounce.
